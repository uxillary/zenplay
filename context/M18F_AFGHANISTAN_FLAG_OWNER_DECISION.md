# Afghanistan flag choice — owner decision

## What the current game shows

`public/flags/af.svg` depicts the black, red, and green vertical tricolour with the state emblem, used under the Islamic Republic and described in Article 19 of the 2004 Constitution, still hosted by the Ministry of Justice ([constitution](https://moj.gov.af/en/enforced-constitution-afghanistan)). The asset is preserved unchanged in M18F and has no memory hook.

## Current representations

The de facto authorities use the white Islamic Emirate flag; the Ministry of Justice reported raising it in 2026 ([Ministry report](https://moj.gov.af/dr/node/4911)). The UN Credentials Committee documented competing representation communications and deferred its decision ([A/78/605](https://documents.un.org/access.nsf/get?DS=A%2F78%2F605&Lang=E&OpenAgent=)). Russia recognised the Taliban government in July 2025 ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/3932240270463715f0338c0812cbe5a8)). These facts concern different questions: constitutional history, territorial control, and international recognition.

## Decision options

1. Keep the existing tricolour and label it as the Islamic Republic era flag.
2. Replace it with the white de facto authorities' flag and label the convention clearly.
3. Present both with dated/contextual labels, which requires product and design work.
4. Keep the existing image temporarily but do not describe it as a verified current flag.

**Recommendation:** choose option 4 until the owner selects the convention the educational quiz should teach. The existing game image remains playable, but the unlabelled quiz should not claim that it is a verified current flag. Add no memory hook until the decision is made.
