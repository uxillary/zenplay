Original flag artwork source: svg-country-flags v1.2.10
Repository: https://github.com/hampusborgos/country-flags
The package metadata uses license "PD" and its README states that the flag designs are public domain. The source notes artwork was taken from Wikimedia Commons and cautions that national flag use may have other restrictions. ZenPlay includes only the 195 country SVGs matching its ISO alpha-2 dataset. No source CSS, JavaScript, or runtime dependency is included.

M18F replacements (accessed 10 October 2026):
- sy.svg: independently drawn from the Syrian Constitutional Declaration (13 March 2025), Article 6 (English translation: https://www.refworld.org/sites/default/files/2026-06/sryia_constituional_declaration.pdf), and Syrian Arab News Agency descriptions of the official flag (https://sana.sy/es/culture/350973/ and https://sana.sy/culture-and-arts/2273533/). The law specifies green, white, and black bands and three red stars; SANA confirms the official flag in current use. Colors are digital approximations from the public-domain Commons flag vector (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Syria.svg); the legal text does not define digital color values. The simple vector geometry is public-domain flag design material; no Commons SVG code was copied.
- kg.svg: independently drawn from Kyrgyz Republic Law No. 208 on State Symbols (https://cbd.minjust.gov.kg/4-5235/edition/953/ru) and Cabinet Resolution No. 724's revised technical specification (https://prg.kz/document/?doc_id=37861395). The specification defines the 5:3 proportion, 40 straight rays, red field, yellow/golden elements, and tunduk dimensions. Flag designs and official symbols are excluded from copyright protection under Kyrgyz Republic copyright law, Article 8. The simplified local SVG uses a geometric reconstruction; it does not copy the Commons SVG source (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Kyrgyzstan.svg).

The national symbols may be subject to country-specific rules on display and use independent of copyright status.
